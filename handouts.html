These are the Amoeba Sisters' own files, unchanged, hosted here so they are one tap away instead of a trip through their website. They are free for study use under their terms: don't edit them, don't sell them, and don't post answer keys publicly. Their name stays on every page, and the videos each sheet goes with are on the plan page. Everything else they make is at amoebasisters.com.

How to use one: open it before the video, fill it in while you watch (pausing is allowed), and a week later print or open it again and fill it in from memory. The second pass is the one that counts.
