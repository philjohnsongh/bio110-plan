How the spacing works

Every card sits in one of four boxes. A new card is in box 0 and is due now. "Got it" moves it up a box and pushes its next appearance out: one day, then three, then seven. "Again" drops it back to box 0 and it returns this round. "Due now" shows only cards whose date has come; "Every card" ignores the dates, which is what you want the night before an exam. Five minutes a day on "Due now" is the whole method; the plan's daily flashcard slot is exactly this.
